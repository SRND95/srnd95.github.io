Capy Shift privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Capy Shift is a game published by Yuzu Studio (independent developer). This page explains which data is used when you play, and why.

What the game does not collect

Yuzu Studio does not collect, store or sell any personal data. The game asks for no name, no email address and no account, and has no server of its own.

Your progress

Your game (coins, items bought with in-game coins, outfit, best score, missions, settings) is saved on your phone. So that you can get it back on a new phone, a copy is sent to your own save space: iCloud (Apple) on iPhone, or Google Play Games "Saved games" on Android. These copies are managed by Apple or Google under their own terms; Yuzu Studio has no access to them.

Leaderboards

If you use Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android), your scores are sent to their leaderboards with your player name. Apple or Google manages this data.

Ads

The game offers ads that you choose to watch (to continue a run or double your coins). They are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID, your approximate IP address and technical information to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalized ads. You can change it at any time.
  • On iPhone, the game asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalized.
  • You can also reset or limit the advertising ID in your phone's settings.

More information: how Google uses data for advertising.

Notifications

The game may schedule at most two reminders on your phone (daily reward, come back). They are local notifications: nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the game settings ("Reminders") or in your phone settings.

Sharing

When you share your score, the picture and the text go to the app you pick (messaging, social network…). Nothing is shared without your action.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are configured to follow the rules that apply to young users.

Your rights

As Yuzu Studio holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. For data managed by Apple or Google (saves, leaderboards, advertising), contact them from your account settings. Uninstalling the game deletes the save stored on the phone.

Changes

This policy may change with the game; the update date is shown at the top of the page.
